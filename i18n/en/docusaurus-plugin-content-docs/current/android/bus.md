Check real-time arrival information by route from the **Bus** tab in the bottom navigation bar.

<img src="/img/android/ko/bus.png" alt="Bus screen" width="320" />

## Route Tabs

- Routes are organized under **Local · Gangnam Station · Suwon Station · Other** tabs.
- The buses expected to arrive at each stop and their remaining times are displayed in real time (scheduled departure times are shown separately as "(Scheduled)").
- Tap **View Full Timetable** to see the complete schedule for that stop, or **Arrival History** to view past arrival records.

## Quick Settings

Tap the **Settings** button at the bottom of the screen to bookmark your frequently used bus routes as favorites, so you can view only your saved routes without switching between tabs.
