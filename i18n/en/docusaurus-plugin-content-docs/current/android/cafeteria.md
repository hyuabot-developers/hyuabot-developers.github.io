Check the menu for each on-campus dining hall from the **Cafeteria** tab in the bottom navigation bar.

<img src="/img/android/ko/cafeteria.png" alt="Cafeteria screen" width="320" />

## Menu Lookup

- The screen is divided into **Breakfast · Lunch · Dinner** tabs.
- You can compare the menus and prices of several on-campus dining halls — including the Faculty Cafeteria, Student Cafeteria, Creative Talent Center Cafeteria, and Business Incubation Center — all on one screen. Tap the info icon next to each dining hall name to check operating hours.
- Use the arrows at the bottom of the screen to navigate to other dates, and tap the **Share** button to share today's meal plan.

:::info
You can also preview today's cafeteria menu by breakfast / lunch / dinner right from the Home screen. Navigate to the Cafeteria tab only when you want to compare menus across all dining halls.
:::
