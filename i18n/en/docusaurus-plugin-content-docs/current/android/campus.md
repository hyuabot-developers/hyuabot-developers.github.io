Access additional features for campus life from the **Campus** tab in the bottom navigation bar.

<img src="/img/android/ko/campus.png" alt="Campus screen" width="320" />

## Campus Tools

- **Campus Map**: Find buildings and classroom locations.
- **Reading Rooms**: Check available seats in each reading room and receive notifications when a seat opens up.
- **Academic Calendar**: View major academic dates at a glance.
- **Campus Contacts**: Look up campus departments and their phone numbers.

## Support & Settings

- **Settings**: Navigate to app preferences for campus, language, theme, and more. See the [Settings](./settings) guide for details.
- **Contact Us**: Leave inquiries about the app in chat form.
- **Donate**, **Rate the App**: Navigate to the relevant external pages.
