Real-time shuttle bus information is integrated into the [Home](./home) screen rather than a separate tab.

## Checking from the Home Screen

- Changing the departure stop selection (e.g., Dormitory → Hanyang University (Ansan) / Arts Center / Jungang Station) refreshes the next departure time and remaining time for that route.
- Subway and bus transfer connections are displayed below the shuttle departure time.
- If location permission is granted, the stop nearest to your current location is selected automatically.

## Full Timetable

Tapping **Full Timetable** on the shuttle card on the Home screen navigates to the complete departure schedule for that stop.

<img src="/img/android/ko/shuttle-timetable.png" alt="Shuttle bus full timetable screen" width="320" />

The timetable is divided into Weekday / Weekend tabs, and you can scroll to view all departure times for the day.

## Classic Shuttle Screen

If you prefer the previous stop-tab style screen, tap **Classic Shuttle Screen** in the [Home Settings](./home#quick-settings) sheet at the bottom of the Home screen to switch to it.

<img src="/img/android/ko/shuttle-legacy-screen.png" alt="Classic shuttle screen" width="320" />

- Stops are listed as tabs at the top (Dormitory, Shuttle Kok, Hanyang University (Ansan), Arts Center, Jungang Station, etc.), and you can switch stops by tapping a tab.
- After switching, the Home tab icon and label in the bottom navigation bar change to **Shuttle Bus**.
- You can switch back to the new Home screen using the **Settings** button at the bottom-right of this screen.
