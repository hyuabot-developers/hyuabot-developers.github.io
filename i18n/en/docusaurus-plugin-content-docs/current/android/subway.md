Check real-time arrival information at Hanyang University (Ansan) Station from the **Subway** tab in the bottom navigation bar.

<img src="/img/android/ko/subway.png" alt="Subway screen" width="320" />

## Real-time Info

- The screen is divided into **Line 4** and **Suinbundang Line** tabs. For each direction (e.g., toward Sadang / Danggogae; toward Ansan / Oido), the time remaining until the next train and stopping information are displayed.
- The **Quick Transfer** tab shows recommended trains that account for transfer walking time.
- Tap **View Full Timetable** to see the complete operating schedule for that direction.
