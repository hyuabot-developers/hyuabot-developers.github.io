You can also check shuttle bus information on Wear OS devices such as Galaxy Watch.

## Features

- **Tile**: Swipe through tiles on the watch face to instantly see the next shuttle departure time.
- **Automatic Stop Detection**: Uses GPS to automatically find and display the stop nearest to your current location.
- **Manual Stop Selection**: If the auto-detected stop is not the one you want, you can select a stop directly in the watch app.

## Installation

If the HYUabot app is installed on the phone paired with your Wear OS device, the watch app is automatically installed via Play Store watch app sync.
