Check real-time arrival information by route from the **Bus** tab in the bottom navigation bar.

<img src="/img/ios/ko/bus.png" alt="Bus real-time screen" width="320" />

## Stop Search

- Search for a stop to look it up, or select one directly from your recently viewed stops list.
- Real-time arrival info for the selected stop is displayed, and you can quickly switch to a nearby alternative stop.

## Timetable & Help

- Navigate to **Timetable** to view the complete departure schedule for that stop.
- The in-screen help section provides usage instructions, and the boarding history section shows past arrival records.
