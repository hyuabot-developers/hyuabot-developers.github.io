Access additional features for campus life from the **Campus** tab in the bottom navigation bar.

<img src="/img/ios/ko/campus.png" alt="Campus screen" width="320" />

## Campus Tools

Navigate directly to the following features from the grid menu.

- **Map**: Check campus building locations.
- **Reading Rooms**: Check available seats in each reading room.
- **Academic Calendar**: View major academic dates.
- **Contacts**: Find campus department contact information.

## Support & Settings

- **Settings**: Navigate to app preferences for campus, language, theme, and more. See the [Settings](./settings) guide for details.
- **Contact Us**: Leave inquiries about the app.
