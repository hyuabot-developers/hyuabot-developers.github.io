Check real-time arrival information by station from the **Subway** tab in the bottom navigation bar.

<img src="/img/ios/ko/subway.png" alt="Subway real-time screen" width="320" />

## Real-time Info

- The time remaining until the next train is shown for each direction (inbound / outbound).
- Line 4, Suinbundang Line, and Seohaeseon Line are supported.

## Timetable

View the complete timetable for each direction on the full operating schedule screen.
