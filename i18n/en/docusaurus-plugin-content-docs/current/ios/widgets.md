Widgets let you check information directly from the home screen and lock screen without opening the app.

## Home Screen Widgets

- **Shuttle Bus Widget**: Shows the next shuttle departure time.
- **Cafeteria Widget**: Shows the on-campus dining hall menu appropriate for the current time of day (morning / lunch / dinner).
- **Transfer Widget**: Shows transfer information connecting bus and subway routes.

## Live Activity

On iOS 16.1 and later, **Shuttle Live Activity** lets you track your shuttle bus ride in real time via the Dynamic Island and the lock screen.

## How to Add

Long-press an empty area on your device's home screen to enter edit mode, tap the **+** button in the top-left corner, find HYUabot, and add the desired widget.
